Using Green Roofs to Reduce Heat Islands

A planted roof can shade the roof surface, cool nearby air through evapotranspiration, and add insulation. EPA distinguishes lighter extensive systems from heavier, more accessible intensive roofs; structural capacity, waterproofing, irrigation, maintenance, stormwater goals, and lifecycle cost matter.
