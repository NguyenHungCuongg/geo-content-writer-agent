Using Trees and Vegetation to Reduce Heat Islands

EPA presents trees and other plants as a natural cooling strategy and points readers to practical guidance for community groups and local governments. Shade and evapotranspiration are part of the cooling mechanisms discussed in its linked heat-island resources.
