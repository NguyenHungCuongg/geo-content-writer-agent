Guide to Reducing Heat Islands

The EPA guide frames heat islands as an interaction among land cover, urban form, material reflectance, emissivity, and heat capacity. It covers health, energy, air-quality, and water impacts, and describes mitigation approaches including vegetation, green roofs, cool roofs, and cool pavements.

Source: U.S. EPA, Guide to Reducing Heat Islands. Guide dated 2008; cool-pavement chapter updated 2012. U.S. federal government work; paraphrased.
