Using Cool Roofs to Reduce Heat Islands

EPA explains that cool roofs combine sunlight reflection with thermal emittance, reducing heat transferred indoors and to surrounding air. Product choice, climate, maintenance, insulation, and winter heating needs affect outcomes; no single roof is optimal everywhere.

Source: U.S. EPA, Using Cool Roofs to Reduce Heat Islands. Updated 2026-06-18. U.S. federal government work; paraphrased.
