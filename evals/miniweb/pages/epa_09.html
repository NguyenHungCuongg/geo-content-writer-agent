Estimating the Environmental Effects of Green Roofs

This EPA case study describes a repeatable framework for assessing environmental and health benefits of green roofs in Kansas City. It combines quantitative tools to help local decision-makers evaluate multiple outcomes rather than treating roof temperature as the only measure.
